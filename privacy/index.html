Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

What we access

When you connect TikTok, we receive your basic profile (display name and avatar) and an access token that lets the app upload videos to your account on your behalf. We do not read your TikTok messages, followers or private data.

How we use it

The token is used only to upload the clips you chose to post (or that you enabled auto-post for). Nothing else is done with your TikTok data.

Where it is stored

The app runs on the user's own computer or server. Tokens, videos and clips are stored there, not shared with third parties and not sold. Transcript text of the videos you clip is sent to the AI service you configured, in order to pick the best moments.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect your TikTok account in the app at any time, which deletes the stored token. You can also revoke access in your TikTok settings. Clips and videos can be deleted from the app.
