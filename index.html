PeakCut Clips

PeakCut Clips turns live streams and long videos into short vertical clips with captions, and can publish them to the creator's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The user connects their own TikTok account (Login Kit, scope user.info.basic).
  2. The app finds the best moments of a stream and renders short vertical clips.
  3. The user chooses to post a clip, or turns on auto-post, and the clip is uploaded to the connected account (Content Posting API, scope video.publish).
